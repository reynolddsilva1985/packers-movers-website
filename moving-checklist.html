A little preparation goes a long way

A simple checklist

Plan early, avoid missed items and make the move-day crew more efficient.

Four to six weeks before

  • Confirm your moving date and both addresses.
  • Walk through every room, loft, garage and shed to create an inventory.
  • Decide what will move, be sold, donated or recycled.
  • Measure large furniture and check access routes, lifts and stairwells.
  • Compare written removal quotes with the same inventory and service scope.

Two to three weeks before

  • Order the right size boxes and packing materials.
  • Start with items you rarely use and label every carton by room.
  • Arrange parking permissions or building lift bookings where required.
  • Notify utilities, council tax, broadband and relevant service providers.
  • Confirm which items need dismantling or special handling.

One week before

  • Finish most packing and keep a separate essentials bag.
  • Keep walkways clear and do not overfill cartons.
  • Set aside keys, documents, medication and valuables for personal transport.
  • Confirm timings, parking, contact arrangements and the written move scope.

On moving day

  • Show the crew items that are not moving.
  • Point out fragile, heavy or unusual items before handling starts.
  • Keep children and pets away from loading areas.
  • Check cupboards, lofts, sheds and storage spaces before leaving.
  • Walk through the destination before unloading is finished.
Cost-saving tip:

Clear labelling, good packing and an accurate inventory reduce wasted time and the risk of extra trips. Do not reduce safety margins by overloading boxes or vehicles.
