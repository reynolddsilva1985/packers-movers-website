# Movers4Less — launch checklist

## Current state
- [x] Responsive multi-page concept website.
- [x] Prominent site-wide notice: early-stage idea, bookings are not open.
- [x] Price guide based on current public market references and initial cost assumptions.
- [x] Browser-only illustrative cost estimator; no contact details collected.
- [x] Enquiry intake page visible but disabled; fields and submit button cannot be used.
- [x] No invented reviews, staff, insurance claims, credentials, phone numbers or business mailbox.
- [x] No analytics or advertising trackers installed by the site code.
- [x] Price book and business plan added for planning.

## Required before accepting the first customer
- [ ] Choose and register the legal business structure and confirm trading name.
- [ ] Confirm service scope, initial postcodes, pricing and capacity.
- [ ] Obtain binding insurance quotations and put suitable cover in force: commercial motor for removals use, goods-in-transit, public liability and employers' liability if employing staff.
- [ ] Confirm payroll and worker status, National Minimum Wage, holiday pay, employer NIC and workplace pension responsibilities.
- [ ] Check each vehicle's gross plated weight, payload, licence requirements and any operator-licence requirements.
- [ ] Obtain written hire terms and actual quotes for vans, parking, materials and any specialist equipment.
- [ ] Create risk assessments and safe working procedures for manual handling, vehicle loading, stairs and specialist lifting.
- [ ] Confirm customer terms, inventory process, cancellation, deposits, payment, claims and complaints handling.
- [ ] Set up a monitored business mailbox and secure form service.
- [ ] Identify the data controller, data processors, privacy contact, retention period and publish a complete privacy notice before data collection.
- [ ] Test enquiry delivery, spam handling, mobile form usability and data deletion.
- [ ] Confirm whether prices are VAT-inclusive/exclusive and display them correctly.
- [ ] Complete a small controlled pilot and compare actual costs against the price book.
- [ ] Obtain real customer feedback before publishing reviews or claims.

## Explicitly not included in the concept launch
- Live bookings, payment taking or form submissions.
- Storage, international removals, piano/safe moves or heavy specialist lifts.
- Guaranteed coverage of every London postcode.
- Claims of existing staff, vehicles, insurance, accreditations or customer reviews.
