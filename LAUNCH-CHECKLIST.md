# Movers4Less — launch and owner decision checklist

This checklist distinguishes work already prepared in the website from matters that cannot responsibly be invented or completed without the business owner.

## Website work completed in this review branch

- [x] Premium responsive visual direction and brand styling.
- [x] Multi-page website structure: home, services, areas, process, about, FAQs, quote preview and privacy information.
- [x] London and surrounding areas positioning, without claiming every postcode is covered.
- [x] Search metadata, canonical URLs, social sharing metadata and MovingCompany structured data on the homepage.
- [x] Mobile navigation, keyboard focus styles, skip link and reduced-motion handling.
- [x] No analytics pixels or advertising scripts added.
- [x] Quote form presented as a disabled preview rather than silently dropping customer details.
- [x] Existing custom-domain file preserved.
- [x] Business blueprint and this checklist added to the repository.

## Owner confirmation required before public launch

### Business identity
- [ ] Confirm legal entity or sole-trader name and the exact trading name.
- [ ] Confirm registration status and any registration details that should be displayed.
- [ ] Confirm the business correspondence address and public-facing contact method.
- [ ] Confirm whether Movers4Less is already operating or is a new business launch.

### Services and operating capability
- [ ] Confirm which of the four proposed service categories can actually be delivered.
- [ ] Confirm whether work is direct, subcontracted or a mixture, and how that is explained to customers.
- [ ] Confirm service-area boundaries, postcode coverage and routes that should be declined.
- [ ] Confirm any limits on load size, item weight, access, floors, stairs and special handling.
- [ ] Confirm availability of packing, materials, dismantling, reassembly and any specialist work.
- [ ] Confirm genuine customer reviews, photographs and any approved case studies.

### Commercial terms and evidence
- [ ] Establish a cost model and approve any published rates or minimum charges.
- [ ] Confirm quoting, survey, deposit, payment, cancellation, delay and rescheduling policies.
- [ ] Confirm insurance policies, policyholder, insured activities, limits and exclusions before making claims.
- [ ] Confirm the complaints and damage-claims process.
- [ ] Obtain permission for any partner logos, customer logos or third-party imagery used.

### Enquiries and privacy
- [ ] Choose an enquiry delivery service or backend and approve the provider.
- [ ] Configure the destination inbox or CRM and test successful delivery end to end.
- [ ] Agree which fields are genuinely necessary.
- [ ] Complete the final privacy notice with controller identity, privacy contact, lawful basis, purposes, recipients, international transfers (if any), retention and rights.
- [ ] Review the hosting and form providers' data processing arrangements.
- [ ] Keep the form disabled until the privacy notice is presented at the point of collection.

### Domain, hosting and launch
- [ ] Review the draft pull request and approve the final source.
- [ ] Confirm GitHub Pages is the intended host or approve an alternative host.
- [ ] Verify DNS and HTTPS for the custom domain after deployment.
- [ ] Test every page, mobile menu, form, internal link, 404 page and metadata.
- [ ] Check Google indexing and Search Console after the site is live.
- [ ] Add an accurate Google Business Profile only after the business details are verified.

## Permission gates

The following actions should not be taken without explicit owner approval:
- Changing DNS or nameservers.
- Moving hosting away from GitHub Pages.
- Creating or connecting paid third-party services.
- Registering a legal business, buying insurance or accepting jobs.
- Publishing unverified contact information, service claims, prices, reviews or credentials.
- Enabling a form that sends customer data to a third party.
- Publishing the site to the production branch before the review is approved.
