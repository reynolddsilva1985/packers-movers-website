These are working price targets for the business idea, based on published UK and London price guides plus an initial operating-cost model. They are not offers, fixed quotes or prices at which a service is currently available.

Concept pricing onlyBookings are not open. These ranges are research estimates to test whether the idea can be affordable and commercially workable. VAT treatment has not yet been set, so these figures are not customer offers or VAT-confirmed prices. A live price would need an inventory, route and access review.

Why separate materials from labour?

Customers who want to pack themselves should not have to pay for a packing team. Itemised materials also make it easier to compare kit sizes and avoid a blanket amount that some homes do not need.

How the market compares

Published UK guides put average three-bedroom home removal costs around £792 (HomeOwners Alliance, using August 2026 data). AnyVan publishes UK-wide sample averages of £483 for a one-bedroom, £555 for a two-bedroom and £688 for a three-bedroom move without packing. A London specialist's 2026 guide based on its own quotes lists £550–£650 for a one-bedroom, £750–£800 for two bedrooms and £800–£1,100 for three bedrooms. These are different samples and service models, so they are reference points rather than direct like-for-like comparisons.

Sources: HomeOwners Alliance 2026 UK removal costs · AnyVan UK home-removal averages · RMV London cost guide · Happy2Move published London prices.

Price promise under consideration

Show the crew and van included, separate optional packing and materials, and disclose parking, waiting-time and access costs before the customer agrees. Do not compete by cutting safety, fair pay or adequate insurance.
