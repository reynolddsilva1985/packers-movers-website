Home / Small moves

Smaller jobs, simpler options

A few items. A whole room.
Help that fits.

Not every move needs a full removals crew. The idea is to offer a smaller, time-based service for jobs that can be completed safely with a suitable van and one or two movers.

One mover + van

Proposed £65 per hour

For straightforward, manageable items where one mover can handle the job safely. Three-hour minimum proposed.

Two movers + van

Proposed £90 per hour

For sofas, beds, appliances and heavier furniture needing two-person handling. Three-hour minimum proposed.

Three movers + van

Proposed £120 per hour

For higher-volume or complex jobs where an extra pair of hands improves safe handling and turnaround. Four-hour minimum proposed.

Common small-move jobs

  • Student belongings and room moves.
  • One or two pieces of furniture.
  • Furniture collected from a retailer or private seller.
  • Small flat or part-load moves.
  • Items going to or from storage.

Final suitability depends on item weight, dimensions, access, distance, loading help and vehicle capacity. The proposed hourly rates are research estimates only, not live offers.
