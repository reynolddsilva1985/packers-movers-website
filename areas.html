Proposed coverage

A London-first idea

The proposed starting point is London and nearby towns. The actual service area would be confirmed by postcode, journey time, crew availability and the cost of returning the vehicle.

West LondonPotential starting locations: Feltham, Hounslow, Ealing, Brentford, Hayes and nearby areas.
South West LondonPotential starting locations: Richmond, Twickenham, Kingston, Wimbledon, Wandsworth and nearby areas.
North and North WestPotential starting locations: Harrow, Wembley, Brent, Barnet and nearby areas.
Central LondonConsidered selectively because parking, loading restrictions and access can change the economics.
South and South EastPotential starting locations: Croydon, Sutton, Bromley, Lewisham and nearby areas.
East LondonPotential starting locations: Stratford, Ilford, Barking, Romford and nearby areas.
These are planning areas only.

No postcode coverage, same-day availability or travel price is being promised. A future operating area should be narrowed to profitable routes and return journeys.
