Local focus

London is the focus.
Your route comes first.

Enquiries are welcome for moves in London and surrounding areas. Share both postcodes so the route and practical requirements can be checked before any quotation is agreed.

Not every route is the same.

Distance is only one part of planning a move. Collection and delivery access, parking, loading restrictions, property layout, timing and the size of the move all affect what can be arranged.

Movers4Less is being developed with a London and nearby-area focus. This page does not claim that every London borough, postcode or surrounding town is automatically covered. Route availability must be confirmed individually.

Before making plans

Do not treat a website enquiry as confirmation of availability. Wait for a written response confirming the route, date, service scope and quotation.
