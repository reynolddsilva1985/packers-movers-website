Home / Declutter and reuse

Keep useful things in use.

The Movers4Less idea starts from a simple problem: people sometimes give away furniture and household belongings because the cost of moving them feels too high. Better planning can help keep useful items in circulation.

Sort belongings into four groups

  • Move: belongings that are useful, valued or expensive to replace.
  • Sell: items in good condition that you no longer need.
  • Donate or give away: useful items that another person or charity can use.
  • Recycle or dispose: damaged or unsafe items that cannot reasonably be reused.

Do not donate something just because moving feels expensive

Before giving away a sofa, bed, table or appliance, compare the cost of moving it with the cost of replacing it. A small additional transport cost may be better value than buying the item again.

Check donation requirements first

Charities have their own rules for furniture condition, fire-safety labels, collection areas and available capacity. Contact the organisation before arranging a drop-off or collection. Do not assume every charity can accept every item.

Reduce the volume before requesting a quote

Remove unwanted items before the inventory is measured. A smaller volume can mean a smaller vehicle, fewer trips or less loading time, which may lower the moving price.
