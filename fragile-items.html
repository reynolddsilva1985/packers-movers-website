Careful handling for heavy and awkward items.

Large, fragile or unusually heavy belongings need a proper assessment of dimensions, weight, access and safe working limits before any move is agreed.

Items needing extra planning

  • Large sofas, wardrobes and oversized furniture.
  • American-style fridge freezers and heavy appliances.
  • Large mirrors, glass tables and artwork.
  • Safes, pianos, aquariums and unusually heavy objects.
  • Items that cannot pass through a doorway, hallway or stairwell.

Equipment that may be used

Depending on the job: furniture dollies, piano skates, lifting straps, floor protection, tail-lift vans, powered stair-climbing trolleys or an external furniture hoist. Any equipment must be suitable for the load and access conditions and used by trained people.

What we would not do

We would not improvise with heavy items, exceed a machine's safe working load, or attempt a job that needs a qualified specialist. Some items should be referred to a specialist mover.

Specialist costs

Quoted after assessment

External furniture hoists, piano moves, safes, cranes and forklift work are not included in standard house-move prices. Third-party equipment and operators would be quoted separately.

Safety first

No assumed capability

Movers4Less is a concept. No specialist equipment, training, operators or insurance are currently claimed.
