Your information

Privacy matters.
So does transparency.

This page describes the current review version of the website. It is not the final privacy notice for a live enquiry service.

Owner action required before launch: identify the legal data controller, publish a working privacy contact, confirm hosting and form providers, set the lawful basis and retention periods, and update this page before enabling collection of customer details. Privacy information must be available when personal data is collected.

1. Current website status

This review version includes a quotation-form design only. Form fields are disabled and information entered into the preview is not sent to Movers4Less or stored by the site's own form code. Do not use the preview to submit real personal or address information.

2. Website hosting

The project is currently configured for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. The final notice should be checked against the hosting arrangement actually used at launch. See GitHub's information about GitHub Pages and its Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and analytics

The site code in this review build does not install analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential tracking cookies. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential storage is added later, the cookie information and consent mechanism must be reviewed before activation. Hosting or third-party service behaviour should also be checked before launch.

4. What must be added before enquiries are enabled

  • The controller's legal or trading identity and postal contact details.
  • A monitored privacy contact address.
  • The purposes and lawful basis for processing enquiry details.
  • The categories of personal data collected and whether any optional details are genuinely necessary.
  • The form processor, hosting providers and any other recipients.
  • Any international transfers and relevant safeguards, if applicable.
  • How long enquiry records are kept and how they are securely deleted.
  • How people can exercise their rights and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

5. Changes to this notice

This page must be updated whenever the website's forms, analytics, hosting, recipients or business practices change. The final notice should be reviewed by the business before live customer data is collected.

Review build date: 7 November 2026.
