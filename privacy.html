Privacy and transparency

This page describes the current concept website and the fact that it does not accept enquiries. It is not a complete privacy notice for a future removals business.

Current position: the enquiry form is disabled. It does not accept, send or store information. Do not enter personal information into the disabled preview.

Website functionality

The site contains static information pages and a local cost estimator. The estimator uses only the selections made in the browser and does not send them to a server. No analytics, advertising pixels or marketing trackers are installed by this site code.

Hosting and server logs

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for security and service operation. Review the hosting provider's current privacy information for details.

Before enquiries are enabled

Before collecting customer names, contact details, addresses or inventories, the business must identify the data controller, choose a secure form provider, set a monitored contact address, explain the purpose and lawful basis, define retention periods, and provide clear information about processors, recipients and people's rights.

Future updates

This page must be replaced with a business-specific privacy notice before a live enquiry form is activated. A privacy contact and company or trading identity must be supplied and checked at that time.
