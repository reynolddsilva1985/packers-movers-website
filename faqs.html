Useful before you enquire

Good questions make
for better planning.

These answers explain the intended enquiry process. The written quotation and agreed service terms take precedence for any actual move.

Which areas do you cover?

Movers4Less is focused on London and surrounding areas. Coverage is confirmed route by route, so include both collection and destination postcodes when you enquire.

How do I get a quotation?

Provide your moving date, both postcodes, the type and approximate size of the move, access details and any additional requirements. A price should only be agreed after the scope is understood and confirmed in writing.

Can I book a move directly through the website?

No. The website is designed to start an enquiry, not take a confirmed booking or payment. Wait for written confirmation of availability, scope, price and terms before making arrangements.

Do you provide packing materials or a packing service?

Packing and preparation are listed as enquiry categories. The exact level of service, materials and availability must be confirmed in the quotation. Do not assume packing materials are included unless stated in writing.

Can you move fragile, valuable or unusually heavy items?

Describe each item before accepting a quotation. Special handling, equipment, restrictions and any insurance conditions need to be confirmed specifically. The website does not currently advertise specialist-item capability.

Are goods in transit or public liability insurance included?

No insurance claim is made on this website at present. Ask the business for current policy details, limits, exclusions and evidence relevant to your move before booking. Do not rely on an assumption that a particular item or risk is covered.

What if my moving date changes?

Any change to date, route, inventory or access should be raised as soon as possible. The effect on availability, price and cancellation terms must be agreed in writing.

Do you offer storage or international removals?

These are not currently advertised as confirmed services. Ask about a specific requirement, and proceed only if the business confirms it can provide the service and includes it in the written agreement.

When is my move confirmed?

An online enquiry is not a booking. A booking should only be treated as confirmed once the business has provided written acceptance and you have agreed the price, dates, inclusions, exclusions and applicable terms.

Still have a question about your move?

Include it with the route and moving details.
