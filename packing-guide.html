Pack the right thing

in the right box

Good packing can reduce breakages and make loading faster. Use suitable materials and keep the weight of each box manageable.

Choose box sizes carefully

  • Small boxes: books, canned food, crockery and dense items. Keep them manageable to lift.
  • Medium boxes: kitchenware, toys, ornaments and general household goods.
  • Large boxes: bedding, cushions, clothing and other light, bulky items.
  • Wardrobe cartons: hanging clothes, where a proper rail and carton are provided.

Protect fragile items

  • Wrap each glass, plate and ornament separately.
  • Use paper or appropriate cushioning to fill empty spaces.
  • Use strong double-wall cartons and seal the base and top.
  • Mark fragile cartons clearly and identify which way up they should travel.
  • Tell the mover about mirrors, glass tops, artwork and electronics before the move.

Keep boxes safe to handle

Do not overfill cartons, leave handles unobstructed or put all heavy objects into one box. Keep liquids sealed and transport important documents, cash, jewellery and essential medication yourself.

Label every carton

Write the destination room on at least two sides. Number cartons if you are using an inventory. Keep a separate note of any boxes containing delicate or time-sensitive items.
