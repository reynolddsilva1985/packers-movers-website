A straightforward process

Clear details first.
Then a considered quote.

A useful quotation starts with an accurate picture of the move. This is the proposed enquiry journey for Movers4Less.

What happens at each stage?

The process below sets out how the website is designed to work. Actual response times, survey arrangements, availability and booking conditions must be confirmed by the business before launch.

A quote is not a booking.

Your move should only be treated as booked when you have received and accepted written confirmation covering the agreed scope, date, price and terms.

01

Share the essentials

Tell us what is moving, the collection and destination postcodes, your preferred date and any access or handling requirements.

02

Clarify the scope

Check the inventory, access, services required, timing and any exclusions. Photos or a survey may be useful where the move is complex, subject to the business process.

03

Review the written quote

Check the price, service inclusions, exclusions, dates, payment terms and cancellation arrangements. Ask questions before accepting.

04

Confirm the booking

Proceed only after written confirmation and agreement to the applicable service terms. Keep the confirmation for reference.
