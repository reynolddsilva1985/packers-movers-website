Clear expectations

Simple steps. Clear expectations.

A future booking journey should be straightforward and transparent. The website currently explains the intended process; it does not take bookings or payments.

01

Tell us what needs moving

Provide a room-by-room inventory, approximate box count, large items, collection and delivery postcodes.

02

Check access and timing

Confirm stairs, lifts, parking, long carries, loading restrictions, move date and flexibility.

03

Choose the service level

Decide whether you will pack yourself, need materials, want fragile-only support or require full packing.

04

Receive a written quote

The quote would show the team, vehicle, services, materials, exclusions, VAT treatment and any additional charges.

05

Confirm and prepare

A booking would only be confirmed after written acceptance of availability, price and terms. Customers would receive preparation guidance.

06

Move and complete

The crew completes the agreed scope, checks the inventory and records any agreed changes before payment is finalised.
