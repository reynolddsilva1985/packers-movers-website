Business moves

Move the workplace
with a clear plan.

A future small-office service could cover desks, chairs, boxed files, shelving and approved office equipment. Large commercial projects and IT infrastructure moves would need a separate specialist scope.

Potential service scope

  • Small office and studio relocations.
  • Desk, chair, shelving and boxed-file moves.
  • Pre-labelled crates and packing materials.
  • Out-of-hours or weekend options, subject to crew and vehicle availability.
  • Basic furniture disassembly and reassembly where agreed.

Planning requirements

Before accepting a workplace move, the scope would need an inventory, building access and loading rules, lift bookings, parking arrangements, delivery windows, insurance requirements and a named site contact.

Server racks, data-centre equipment, heavy machinery, hazardous materials and high-value specialist equipment should be excluded unless a qualified partner is engaged and the risk is assessed.
