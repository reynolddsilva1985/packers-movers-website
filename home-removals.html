Proposed residential service

A more considered way
to move home.

A future home-removal service should be sized around what you own, not just the number of bedrooms. The aim is a clear scope, a suitable crew and a fair price.

What a home move could include

Collection, loading, transport and unloading of household furniture and packed belongings. Optional help could include packing, moving boxes, agreed furniture dismantling and reassembly, and protection for floors and doorways.

How the move would be planned

  • Record items and approximate volume to be moved.
  • Check both addresses for stairs, lifts, parking, long carries and narrow access.
  • Choose the right vehicle and crew size.
  • Agree what is included, excluded and any additional charges in writing.

What affects the price?

Home size is a starting point only. Volume, route, crew hours, number of trips, stairs, parking, special items, packing and dismantling can change the cost.
