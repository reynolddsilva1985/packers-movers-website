The idea

Good belongings should not become
disposable because moving is expensive.

The aim is to make moving more predictable and reasonably priced. Customers should be able to choose the level of help they need, understand what each option costs and avoid paying for services they do not want.

Built around choice

A move should fit your needs,
not the other way around.

The Movers4Less idea is to make the cost of moving easier to understand by separating the help you need from the extras you may not.
