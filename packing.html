Part-packing

Fragile-only packing

Kitchen glassware, ornaments, mirrors and selected delicate items could be packed as a separate service. Inventory and materials need checking first.

Full packing

Whole-home packing

Packers prepare agreed contents of a home ahead of moving day. Materials would be listed separately so the customer can see the total.

Proposed packing labour

Working price target: £65–£75 per hour for a two-person packing team, with a three-hour minimum, plus the materials actually used. A fixed package could be offered only after the quantity of belongings and fragile items is understood.

Why boxes matter

Heavy items such as books should go in smaller cartons. Large cartons should be reserved for lighter items such as linen and cushions. Double-wall cartons, sound tape and suitable wrapping help reduce damage risk.
